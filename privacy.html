Your information

Privacy, kept clear.

This policy explains what JDH Studio receives when you contact the studio or submit a review.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Information you send

When you use the contact or review forms, you may send your name, email address, organisation, message, rating, project name, and any other information you choose to include. These forms are processed by Formspree and delivered to JDH Studio.

How it is used

JDH Studio uses enquiry information to respond to requests, understand project needs, provide support, and keep a record of business conversations. Review submissions may be edited for clarity and are manually approved for public display only when the reviewer gives permission.

Third parties

This website uses Formspree for form delivery, Google Fonts for typography, WhatsApp for messages, Instagram for social links, and Google Forms for the discovery questionnaire. Those services have their own privacy policies and may process data according to their terms.

Retention and choices

JDH Studio keeps information only as long as reasonably needed for communication, project delivery, records, or legal obligations. To ask about information you submitted or request its correction or deletion, email jdhstudio.co@gmail.com.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not intentionally set advertising cookies or use a tracking analytics platform. External services linked from this site may use their own cookies when opened.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site or its services change. The date above shows when it was last reviewed.
