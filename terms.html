Working together

Clear terms for good work.

These are the general terms for using the JDH Studio website and starting a project conversation.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Website information

This website describes JDH Studio services and selected work. It is provided for general information and does not create a client relationship or guarantee a particular result.

Project agreements

Every paid project should have a written agreement or approved proposal describing the scope, deliverables, timing, responsibilities, payment terms, revisions, hosting, ownership, and support. The work and budget depend on the actual requirements.

Client responsibilities

Clients are responsible for providing accurate information, access, approvals, content, licences, and timely feedback needed for the agreed work. Delays in those items may affect delivery timing.

Third-party services

Projects may use hosting providers, domains, payment platforms, APIs, automation tools, or other third-party services. Their availability, pricing, policies, and performance may be outside JDH Studio’s control.

Reviews and permissions

By submitting a review, you confirm that it reflects your experience. Selecting “Yes” in the permission field allows JDH Studio to manually approve and publish the review. A review is not published with a person’s name, company, project details, or image without appropriate permission.

Intellectual property

Ownership and usage rights for project designs, code, content, assets, and third-party materials should be stated in the written project agreement. You must have permission to provide material you ask JDH Studio to use.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to jdhstudio.co@gmail.com.

These plain-language terms are a starting point, not legal advice. Have a qualified adviser review them before relying on them for client contracts or regulatory compliance.
